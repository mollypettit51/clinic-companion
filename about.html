Behind the project

Built from real classroom needs.

Clinic Companion began as a practical idea: make basic school-health information easier for teachers and school staff to access while keeping medical assessment and decision-making with the school nurse.

The story

Why I created Clinic Companion

As a school nurse, I frequently see students visit the clinic for minor complaints that may sometimes be managed initially with permitted classroom comfort measures. Teachers are also often unsure whether a particular concern should be referred to the nurse. I wanted to create an accessible resource that supports staff as they navigate those everyday decisions.

I developed Clinic Companion as my custom chatbot for Lab 3 in SMU's Generative AI for Business class. It uses school-health reference materials to provide clear, simple information on topics like headaches, stomachaches, nosebleeds, minor injuries, and colds. The goal is to help staff find general guidance quickly, recognize warning signs, and know when to contact the nurse.

For Lab 5, I expanded the idea into this website. It brings together information about the project, access to the chatbot, and an interactive quick-reference guide in one easy-to-use place.

How it works

Simple by design.

01

Ask a question

A teacher or staff member asks Clinic Companion a general question about a student-health concern without sharing identifying details.

02

Review the guidance

The chatbot draws on its school-health knowledge base to provide practical information and help staff recognize when nurse involvement may be needed.

03

Follow school procedures

Staff use the information alongside school procedures, student-specific health plans, and the guidance of authorized health personnel.

What's inside the knowledge base

Focused on everyday school health.

The chatbot's reference material covers three core areas: common student health complaints, when to contact the school nurse, and general school clinic procedures.

Safety and privacy are central to the design. Clinic Companion does not diagnose, prescribe medication, ask for confidential student details, or replace emergency action plans. When an answer is unclear, staff should contact the nurse.
